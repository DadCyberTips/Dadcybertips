[ PRIVACY POLICY ]

💚 Your privacy is sacred. We protect it with the same care we teach families to protect themselves online.

[ WHAT INFORMATION WE COLLECT ]

When you interact with DadCyberTips, we may collect:

  • Contact Form Data: Name, email, phone (optional), organization (optional), and message content when you contact us
  • Quiz Responses: Your quiz answers and security assessment scores to provide personalized recommendations
  • Account Data: Information you provide when purchasing books or services (handled by third-party vendors like Payhip and Fourthwall)
  • Automatic Information: General usage data through GitHub Pages analytics (no personally identifying information)

[ HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION ]

We use your information for these purposes only:

  • 📧 Respond to inquiries: To reply to your contact form submissions and questions
  • 🎯 Provide recommendations: To deliver personalized cybersecurity advice based on your quiz results
  • 📚 Process requests: To handle book purchases, service bookings, and other transactions
  • 🛡️ Improve content: To understand what topics interest you so we can create better resources
  • 🔐 Security: To protect against fraud and abuse

[ WE NEVER SELL YOUR DATA ]

✋ YOUR DATA IS YOURS ALONE. WE WILL NEVER SELL, RENT, OR TRADE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION TO THIRD PARTIES.

We may share data only with:

  • Essential Service Providers: Payment processors, email platforms, and database services that help us operate (all bound by confidentiality)
  • Legal Requirements: Only when required by law or to protect safety and rights

[ HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR DATA ]

  • Quiz Data: Retained for 1 year to help us improve the quiz and your experience
  • Contact Form Data: Retained as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, then archived for 2 years
  • Purchase Information: Retained per merchant requirements and applicable law

[ REQUEST DATA REMOVAL ]

You have the right to request that we delete all your personal data from our systems. To request removal:

Fill out our contact form and select "Request Data Removal" from the Category dropdown.

Include any relevant email addresses or account identifiers, and we'll process your request within 30 days.

[ SECURITY ]

We take data security seriously. All data collection happens over secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS). We use industry-standard practices to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, and disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

[ YOUR RIGHTS ]

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we have about you
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data
  • Opt-out: Decline to provide certain optional information

[ THIRD-PARTY SERVICES ]

We use third-party platforms for specific functions:

  • GitHub Pages: Hosts our website (privacy policy: github.com/privacy)
  • Payhip: Processes book sales (privacy policy: payhip.com/privacy)
  • Fourthwall: Manages merchandise store (privacy policy: fourthwall.com/privacy)
  • Make.com: Handles form submissions and automation (privacy policy: make.com/privacy)
  • Notion: Stores contact and quiz data (privacy policy: notion.so/privacy)

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them, as we are not responsible for their practices.

[ CHILDREN'S PRIVACY ]

DadCyberTips is designed for families. If a parent or guardian, you consent to the collection of information from children under your supervision. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without parental consent.

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of DadCyberTips after changes means you accept the updated policy.
